Open Laptop

Fine-grained specification9 branches117 test episodes
Same initial state, different instructions (scene 0, expert demonstrations).
InstructionOpen the laptop to 45|90|135 degrees by contacting the left|center|right region of the lid.
DescriptionA closed laptop is placed in front of the robot. The instruction specifies the final opening angle and the region of the lid that the gripper should push on. The angle is measured from the simulated hinge joint, and the contact location from recorded contacts.
Variables
  • Opening angle: 45°, 90°, 135°
  • Contact region: left, center, right
  • Arm: not specified by the instruction; assigned by the task
Branches9 per scene, all from the same initial scene and robot state
CategoryFine-grained specification
PlatformSimulation · RoboTwin 2.0
Data342 train / 117 val / 117 test episodes · 64 eligible of 87 candidate scenes
ExpertMulti-branch expert, 2 refinement iterations · 73.6% scene eligible rate
Action budget700 actions
UsageTrain & Eval (test_clear) / Random Eval (test_random)

Fidelity checker

Checkers score simulator states and execution records (trajectories, contacts, activation events). Policies never see their output. Episodes run for the fixed action budget, followed by 250 passive physics steps, and are scored with no early stop on success.

Task success (TR)Requested opening angle reached; lid stable; joint within legal limits.
Joint success (JR)TR, plus: Contact the requested lid region.

Criteria as stated in the paper (Appendix, Table “Canonical instructions and episode-level success criteria”).

CheckGroupCondition
target_angle_reachedTRThe final lid angle is within ± 8° of the requested angle.
lid_stableTRThe hinge velocity is below the stability threshold.
joint_within_limitsTRThe hinge position stays within its legal joint limits.
requested_contact_regionJRThe measured lid contact falls in the requested region (left / center / right) and lies within 3.5 cm of its center.
active_arm_contactJRThe task-assigned arm contacts the lid.
inactive_arm_no_contactJRThe other arm does not contact the laptop.

The benchmark checker computes TR as all 3 TR checks, and JR as TR plus all 3 JR checks. Check names and grouping follow the frozen evaluation protocol roboinf.paper_table.origin_archive.v2.
