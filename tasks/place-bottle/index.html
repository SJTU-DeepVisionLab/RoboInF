Place Bottle

Goal selectionProcedural adherence8 branches104 test episodes
Same initial state, different instructions (scene 3, expert demonstrations).
InstructionMove bottle_left|bottle_right to goal_left|goal_right using the left|right arm.
DescriptionTwo bottles stand on the table in front of two goal pads. The instruction selects which bottle to move, which pad to place it on, and which arm to use. The other bottle must remain standing.
Variables
  • Bottle: left, right
  • Goal: left, right
  • Arm: left, right
Branches8 per scene, all from the same initial scene and robot state
CategoryGoal selection · Procedural adherence
PlatformSimulation · RoboTwin 2.0
Data296 train / 104 val / 104 test episodes · 63 eligible of 287 candidate scenes
ExpertMulti-branch expert, 10 refinement iterations · 21.9% scene eligible rate
Action budget400 actions
UsageTrain & Eval (test_clear) / Random Eval (test_random)

Fidelity checker

Checkers score simulator states and execution records (trajectories, contacts, activation events). Policies never see their output. Episodes run for the fixed action budget, followed by 250 passive physics steps, and are scored with no early stop on success.

Task success (TR)Target bottle center inside the requested goal; target bottle upright; active gripper open.
Joint success (JR)TR, plus: Use the requested arm; keep the non-target bottle undisturbed and upright.

Criteria as stated in the paper (Appendix, Table “Canonical instructions and episode-level success criteria”).

CheckGroupCondition
target_center_inside_goalTRThe x–y position of the requested bottle's functional center lies within the half-extent of the requested goal.
target_bottle_uprightTRThe requested bottle's center height is at most 2 cm below its upright resting height.
active_gripper_openTRThe gripper of the active arm is open.
requested_arm_contactJRThe requested arm makes contact with the target bottle during the episode.
passive_bottle_uprightJRThe non-target bottle's center height is at most 2 cm below its upright resting height.

The benchmark checker computes TR as all 3 TR checks, and JR as TR plus all 2 JR checks. Check names and grouping follow the frozen evaluation protocol roboinf.paper_table.origin_archive.v2.
