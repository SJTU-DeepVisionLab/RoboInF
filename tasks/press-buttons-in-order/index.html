Press Buttons in Order

Procedural adherence12 branches108 test episodes
Same initial state, different instructions (scene 7, expert demonstrations).
InstructionUsing the left|right arm, press the left|middle|right, …, and … buttons in that order.
DescriptionThree buttons have to be pressed with one arm. All branches end with every button ON, so the final state is identical; the press order and the arm are checked from the recorded activation events.
Variables
  • Arm: left, right
  • Order: all 6 permutations of left, middle, right
Branches12 per scene, all from the same initial scene and robot state
CategoryProcedural adherence
PlatformSimulation · RoboTwin 2.0
Data336 train / 108 val / 108 test episodes · 46 eligible of 66 candidate scenes
ExpertMulti-branch expert, 10 refinement iterations · 69.7% scene eligible rate
Action budget600 actions
UsageTrain & Eval (test_clear) / Random Eval (test_random)

Fidelity checker

Checkers score simulator states and execution records (trajectories, contacts, activation events). Policies never see their output. Episodes run for the fixed action budget, followed by 250 passive physics steps, and are scored with no early stop on success.

Task success (TR)All three switches ON at completion.
Joint success (JR)TR, plus: Measured activation sequence equals the requested button permutation; all presses use the requested arm.

Criteria as stated in the paper (Appendix, Table “Canonical instructions and episode-level success criteria”).

CheckGroupCondition
all_switches_onTRAll three buttons end ON (progress ≥ 0.95) in the final sample.
initially_all_offJRAll buttons start OFF (progress ≤ 0.10).
requested_press_orderJRThe sequence of ON transitions equals the requested order, and no two buttons turn on simultaneously.
correct_arm_contactJREvery activation is made by the requested arm's hand, and the other arm never touches a button.
inactive_arm_stationaryJRThe other arm stays still over the full physics history: six arm joints drift ≤ 0.06 rad, two finger joints ≤ 0.03 m.
inter_arm_collision_freeJRThe two arms never collide.
released_and_stableJROver the final settle window every button stays ON, the hand has moved clear, and nothing is touching the buttons.

The benchmark checker computes TR as all 1 TR checks, and JR as TR plus all 6 JR checks. Check names and grouping follow the frozen evaluation protocol roboinf.paper_table.origin_archive.v2.
