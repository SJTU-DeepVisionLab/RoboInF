Shake Bottle

Procedural adherenceFine-grained specification8 branches104 test episodes
Same initial state, different instructions (scene 1, expert demonstrations).
InstructionUse the left|right arm to hold the bottle horizontally|vertically and shake it left and right three times|with three small wrist rotations around the horizontal grasp axis.
DescriptionThe robot picks up a bottle and shakes it. Branches differ in the arm, the holding pose, and the motion pattern (lateral translation or wrist rotation). The bottle reaches roughly the same place in every branch, so only the execution reveals whether the instruction was followed.
Variables
  • Arm: left, right
  • Holding pose: horizontal, vertical
  • Motion: lateral translation ×3, wrist rotation ×3
Branches8 per scene, all from the same initial scene and robot state
CategoryProcedural adherence · Fine-grained specification
PlatformSimulation · RoboTwin 2.0
Data296 train / 104 val / 104 test episodes · 63 eligible of 75 candidate scenes
ExpertMulti-branch expert, 8 refinement iterations · 84.0% scene eligible rate
Action budget700 actions
UsageTrain & Eval (test_clear) / Random Eval (test_random)

Fidelity checker

Checkers score simulator states and execution records (trajectories, contacts, activation events). Policies never see their output. Episodes run for the fixed action budget, followed by 250 passive physics steps, and are scored with no early stop on success.

Task success (TR)Bottle contacted and held, with the gripper closed at completion; requested final holding pose.
Joint success (JR)TR, plus: Hold and shake with the requested arm; maintain the requested holding pose during shaking; execute exactly three cycles of the requested motion: left–right shaking or small wrist rotations around the horizontal grasp axis.

Criteria as stated in the paper (Appendix, Table “Canonical instructions and episode-level success criteria”).

CheckGroupCondition
bottle_heldTRThe bottle is held by the robot in the final physics sample.
final_gripper_closedTRThe gripper is closed on the bottle at completion (final holding measurement).
pose_after_shakeTRAfter shaking, the bottle stays within the pose tolerance of the requested holding pose (horizontal / vertical).
active_arm_contactJRThe requested arm contacts the bottle.
inactive_arm_no_contactJRThe other arm never contacts the bottle.
inactive_arm_stationaryJRThe other arm stays still over the full physics history: six arm joints drift ≤ 0.06 rad, two finger joints ≤ 0.03 m.
pose_during_shakeJRWhile shaking, the bottle stays within the pose tolerance of the requested holding pose.
trace_three_cyclesJRThe measured motion trace contains exactly three cycles.
trace_amplitudeJRPeak-to-peak amplitude of the trace is ≥ 2.5 cm (left–right shaking) or ≥ 16° (wrist rotation).
lifted_and_heldJRThe bottle stays above the lift clearance during the shake, settle and completion phases.
wrist_position_stableJRWrist rotation only: the gripper (TCP) drifts ≤ 3 cm while rotating. Not applicable to left–right shaking.
wrist_axis_horizontalJRWrist rotation only: the rotation axis stays close to horizontal. Not applicable to left–right shaking.
unintended_motion_limitedJRNo unrequested motion: orientation drift is limited during left–right shaking, and bottle-center drift is limited during wrist rotation.

The benchmark checker computes TR as all 3 TR checks, and JR as TR plus all 10 JR checks. Check names and grouping follow the frozen evaluation protocol roboinf.paper_table.origin_archive.v2.
